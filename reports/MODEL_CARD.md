## الغرض والاستخدام | Purpose and use
This is a fictional training project using synthetic loan applications to rank the risk of a simulated default within 90 days and examine a limited review queue. The loss of 10 * FN + FP and the 12% cap are teaching assumptions, not validated financial costs or staffing requirements. The artifact is intended for coursework, reproducible experiments and review of modeling decisions. It must not be used to approve or reject real loans, set loan prices or credit limits, or generate customer-facing adverse-action reasons. The results do not establish production readiness or suitability for real lending.
بيانات Tamweel Lite اصطناعية؛ الهدف حدث خلال 90 يومًا بعد الطلب. 22 خاصية متاحة وقت الطلب؛ لا معرفات أو تواريخ أو هدف في المدخلات. الاستخدام التعليمي فقط؛ لا قرارات تمويل فعلية.
## البيانات والتحقق | Data and validation
حوض التدريب والاختيار: 6576 طلبًا. المعايرة: 836 طلبًا و78 موجبًا. حجز عملاء المعايرة، 90 يومًا لنضج التسميات، وOOF أمامي متداخل مع فصل العملاء في المستويين. طيات المقارنة: 2023Q1 و2023Q3 و2024Q1. نستبعد النتائج التي لم تنضج قبل الأدوار التالية؛ آخر بيانات التدريب لا تستخدم تلقائيًا.
The nested forward OOF comparison produced 2,155 LIVE predictions across three forward periods. The design separates inner ensemble fitting from outer scoring and addresses time ordering, customer separation and 90-day label availability. Warm-up rows have no outer OOF predictions. However, these outer predictions were used to choose the candidate and its policy threshold, so the reported results are selection evidence and still require a separate final evaluation. Three folds provide limited evidence about temporal stability, and fold SD is not a confidence interval. The data are synthetic and have already been explored during the course. The 2,500 challenge requests have no labels, so they cannot establish predictive performance.
## النموذج والقرار | Model selection
KEEP SINGLE / Logistic. انحراف AP المرجعي: 0.029808. ارجع إلى artifacts/ensemble_comparison.csv وday5_fold_scores.csv للأرقام الكاملة.
I kept Logistic Regression because it had the highest mean outer-fold AP (0.39166, fold SD 0.02981). The best ensemble by AP was Weighted (0.38942, SD 0.02906), so its AP was lower by 0.00224. Logistic also had a slightly lower raw OOF Brier score (0.06327 versus 0.06332), although Weighted had a lower ECE (0.01772 versus 0.01882). Equal averaging and stacking had lower AP, at 0.37170 and 0.38314. No ensemble passed the worth-it gate. These small differences do not establish statistical superiority, but they provide no evidence of an AP gain that justifies maintaining multiple models, blend weights or a stacking model.
## المعايرة | Calibration
Sigmoid على عينة محجوزة من التدريب والاختيار؛ الرسم والمقاييس تشخيص على عينة تعلم المعاير، وليسا اختبارًا مستقلاً. لا ادعاء بتحسن على تحدٍّ مجهول التسميات.
The final classifier used 6,576 fitting/selection rows, while sigmoid calibration used a separate 836 rows with 78 positives. Those 836 rows were held out from classifier training, but the calibration mapping was scored on the same rows used to fit it. These are calibration-fit diagnostics, not an independent test of calibration. In this run, sigmoid worsened Brier from 0.076473 to 0.078058, log loss from 0.266489 to 0.277296, and ECE from 0.021121 to 0.034871. AP remained 0.287803 and ROC-AUC remained 0.789037. I cannot claim a calibration benefit. The exported mapping needs evaluation on a separate later labeled cohort before deciding whether to keep it.
## السياسة والسعة والمناطق | Policy and regions
خسارة 10 FN + FP، عتبة OOF الخام 0.16892161427109176 والمنقولة 0.12225843144286948. سعة الدفعة 300؛ المرشحون 330؛ الإشارات النهائية 300. كتلة الدرجات المتساوية لا تقسم. 1=إشارة مراجعة تعليمية، 0=عدم رفع الإشارة.
The 12% cap was applied once to the complete batch of 2,500 challenge requests, giving a capacity of 300. At the transported threshold of 0.122258, 330 requests were eligible; the cap retained 300 and removed 30. These removed requests are not known false positives because challenge labels are unavailable. Regional OOF FPR uses actual negatives as the denominator: central 39/489 (7.98%), western 50/486 (10.29%), eastern 32/507 (6.31%), and other 40/494 (8.10%). The largest gap is 3.98 percentage points. Recall uses actual positives, with only 35-49 positives per region. These are OOF threshold diagnostics, not post-cap challenge outcomes. Capacity compliance does not establish fairness. I need regional uncertainty checks and an audit of selection rates and cap removals, followed by outcome-based checks once labels mature.
## التفسير وحدوده | Explanation scope
تفسير اليوم الرابع يخص نموذج اليوم الرابع؛ لا يُنسب تلقائيًا إلى هذه النسخة. تغيير النموذج أو خصائصه أو معايرته يستلزم مراجعة التفسير.
Day 4 explains a weighted LightGBM fitted on 2,516 rows, while Day 5 selected Logistic Regression and refit it on 6,576 rows. The earlier SHAP plots, permutation importance and reason codes therefore do not explain the final model. I need new explanations for the frozen Logistic pipeline using its actual preprocessing and an appropriate explainer and background. I would verify output units and additivity, recheck imputation, reason codes and local stability, and assess permutation importance on suitable held-out data. The final sigmoid mapping and threshold-plus-cap policy also need separate checks. A feature explanation of a score does not explain why a threshold-eligible request was removed by the cap. Explanations describe learned associations and do not establish causality or fairness.
## المتابعة والقيود | Monitoring and limitations
For each batch, I would check the schema, feature availability, missingness, duplicate IDs, feature distributions and score drift. I would log total requests, threshold eligibility, final flags and cap removals, enforce the 12% limit over the full batch, and compare selection and removal rates by region. When 90-day labels mature, I would check default prevalence, AP, recall, precision, 10 * FN + FP, Brier, log loss, ECE and reliability-bin counts. Regional outcome checks must report positive and negative denominators and uncertainty. Sustained drift or deteriorating results would trigger investigation. Any model, calibration or policy revision should be developed on designated data and assessed on a fresh later cohort before replacing the frozen procedure. A model refit also requires updated explanations.
